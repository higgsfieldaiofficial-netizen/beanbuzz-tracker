Hisab Logic

COGS = Opening Stock + Stock Khareed − Closing Stock. Gross Profit = Sale − COGS − Fresh kharcha − Wastage. Net Profit = Gross − Operating kharcha (Rapido, gas, staff…). Pura Net Profit Abhinash ka hai — abhi koi partner nahi hai. Mahine ke end me Stock tab me Closing Stock daalte hi asli profit pakka ho jata hai — usse pehle dashboard cash hisab dikhata hai.
